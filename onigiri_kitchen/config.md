**Onigiri Kitchen settings**

Most of these can also be changed from the timer panel inside the kitchen (the dango button).

- `focus_minutes`: length of a focus session.
- `short_break_minutes` / `long_break_minutes`: break lengths.
- `rounds_before_long_break`: focus sessions per cycle (one dango each). The long break comes after the last one.
- `focus_card_goal`: end focus early after this many reviews (`0` = time only).
- `daily_card_goal`: reach this many reviews in a day and your restaurant throws a little party with fireworks the next time you visit (default `100`, `0` = off).
- `auto_open_kitchen_on_break`: open the restaurant automatically when a break starts.
- `auto_start_next_focus`: start the next focus session as soon as a break ends.
- `widget_click_opens_kitchen`: clicking the restaurant picture on Onigiri's main screen opens the kitchen. Shift-click still expands the widget the way Onigiri normally does.
- `show_timer_chip` / `show_timer_chip_in_reviewer`: show the small dango timer in the corner.
- `reviews_per_guest`: how many reviews in a deck bring one guest to your restaurant.
- `sound` / `volume`: soft koto sounds, chimes and meows (0.0–1.0).
- `shortcut`: keyboard shortcut for Tools → Onigiri Kitchen (restart Anki after changing it).
- `show_home_widget_without_onigiri`: without Onigiri, show the kitchen widget under the deck list on Anki's main screen. With Onigiri, place the widget with Onigiri's own layout editor instead (Onigiri settings → main menu layout; it's listed as "Onigiri Kitchen · おにぎり食堂").
- `celebrate_deck_finish`: confetti and a little fanfare when you finish a deck (the "Congratulations" screen right after reviewing). It's skipped if your system has "reduce motion" turned on.
- `pause_focus_when_idle` / `idle_pause_minutes`: the focus countdown pauses after this many minutes without reviewing (and gives that idle time back), then resumes when you answer your next card. Turn it off if you use the timer for studying outside Anki.
