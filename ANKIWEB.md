## Description (paste into the description box)

```
<b>A cozy pixel-art restaurant, pomodoro timer and shop cat. Made as a companion to <a href="https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1011095603">Onigiri</a>.</b>

You study, guests arrive. You take a break, you get to enjoy them.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thussenthan/onigiri-kitchen/main/docs/day.png" width="720">

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li><b>A living pixel restaurant.</b> Click Onigiri's restaurant widget to open it. Lanterns, a noren curtain in your theme colour, and a window onto Mt. Fuji that follows your real clock and the seasons.</li>
<li><b>Guests come from studying.</b> Every 10 reviews in a deck brings a guest from that deck, and each deck has its own look. Getting a leech right sends a grumpy sour-plum guest who cheers up once fed.</li>
<li><b>Pomodoro timer.</b> One dango per focus session. The restaurant opens when your break starts, and nudges you back when it ends. Long breaks are festival nights with fireworks.</li>
<li><b>Tama, the shop cat.</b> A gentle Tamagotchi-style pet. Studying feeds her, and she grows with total study days (never streaks), develops a personality, learns tricks and brings you gifts. She can't get sick, run away or die.</li>
<li><b>Decor.</b> Guests tip in mon. Spend it on a bonsai, wind chime, maneki-neko, daruma, goldfish and more.</li>
<li><b>Matches your Onigiri theme</b>: your colours, font and dark mode.</li>
</ul>

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thussenthan/onigiri-kitchen/main/docs/night.png" width="720">

<b>How to open it</b>
Click the restaurant picture on Onigiri's main screen (Shift-click keeps Onigiri's normal view), click the shop-front icon next to Onigiri's buttons, click the dango timer in the corner, or use Tools → Onigiri Kitchen (Ctrl+Shift+K).

<b>Your data</b>
Onigiri Kitchen never modifies Onigiri. It only reads your restaurant name, level and theme. Your XP and Taiyaki coins are untouched. Onigiri is recommended but not required.

<b>Bugs & ideas</b>
Use ⚙ → Report a bug inside the kitchen, or open an issue on <a href="https://github.com/thussenthan/onigiri-kitchen">GitHub</a>. Please report problems there rather than in AnkiWeb reviews, since I can't reply to reviews.
```
